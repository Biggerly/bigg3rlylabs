Common Questions

How long does a project take?
Simple landing pages take 1–2 weeks. Full web apps typically range from 4–10 weeks depending on complexity. We'll give you a clear timeline after the discovery call.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes! We work with clients across the UK, US, Canada, and beyond. Communication is fully remote via WhatsApp, Slack, or email — whatever works for you.
What's your payment structure?
We typically work on a 50% upfront, 50% on delivery model. For larger projects we can split into milestones. We accept NGN and USD.
Do you offer post-launch support?
Yes — all projects include a 30-day post-launch support window. We also offer monthly maintenance retainers for clients who need ongoing updates.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Absolutely. Redesigns are one of our specialties — we audit your current site, identify weak points, and rebuild with a modern, performance-first approach.
